## Brand & Style

### Personality & Tone
The design system manifests the intellectual reverence, stillness, and tactile craftsmanship of archival libraries and historic manuscript repositories. It bridges seventeenth-century bookbinding traditions with institutional digital cataloging, balancing curatorial gravity with ergonomic speed. The tone is deliberate, erudite, and protective of historical knowledge.

### Target Audience
Archivists, conservators, paleographers, academic researchers, and rare book curators managing incunabula, codices, and special collections alongside general catalog holdings.

### Design Aesthetic & Movement
An **Editorial-Tactile Historical** movement. The aesthetic avoids nostalgic pastiche through precise typographic scale, disciplined grid mechanics, and structured archival metadata tables. Surfaces replicate archival parchment, vellum, and hand-tooled morocco leather, complemented by discrete burnished brass rules, catalog embossing metaphors, and bespoke *ex libris* architectural devices.

## Colors

### Palette Strategy
- **Primary (`#1B3B2B` — British Library Green):** Sourced from archival library reading desk baize and morocco leather spine bindings. Reserved for navigational anchors, verified state confirmations, and primary action controls.
- **Secondary (`#6A1A24` — Deep Burgundy / Bookbinder's Oxblood):** Derived from marbled book edges and aged calfskin covers. Serves as secondary interaction focal points, restricted archival access markers, and high-importance contextual badges.
- **Tertiary (`#B88E3E` — Gilt Foil / Burnished Brass):** Applied sparingly to micro-rules, monogram seals, metadata indices, active tabs, and delicate focus indicators.
- **Neutral (`#F4EFE6` — Vellum Parchment):** The foundation canvas, providing an aged yet luminous warm ivory surface that eliminates harsh digital eye-strain during prolonged reading sessions.

### Functional Surfaces & Tokens
- **Canvas Base:** `#F4EFE6` (Warm Parchment)
- **Layer 1 Surface (Shelves/Cards):** `#FCFAF6` (Bleached Rag Paper)
- **Layer 2 Surface (Drawers/Modals):** `#EAE3D2` (Aged Manilla / Heavy Vellum)
- **Ink Primary (Headings/Body):** `#1F1A17` (Iron Gall Black-Brown)
- **Ink Secondary (Metadata/Labels):** `#595149` (Distressed Sepia)
- **Ink Subtle (Rules/Borders):** `#D8CEBC` (Deckle Edge / Parchment Tone)
- **Ex Libris Seal Tint:** `rgba(106, 26, 36, 0.08)` (Oxblood Inkwash)
- **Restricted Access Warning:** `#8C2D19` (Vermilion Pigment)

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy & Role Separation
- **Display & Headings (EB Garamond):** Used exclusively for archival manuscript titles, library classifications, department headers, and catalog item nomenclature. Evokes handset renaissance typography and incunabula folios.
- **Longform & Body (Source Serif 4):** Chosen for condition reports, historical marginalia transcriptions, and provenance notes. Offers exceptional optical balance and open apertures that perform gracefully against parchment-toned backgrounds.
- **Technical Metadata & Micro-Labels (Inter):** Applied in uppercase tracking (`+0.06em` to `+0.1em`) for Universal Decimal Classifications (UDC), Dewey codes, call numbers, acquisition barcodes, shelf tags, and data tables. Creates an intentional, modern contrast against the classical serifs, anchoring the interface as a precision working tool.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model
The layout adheres to a structured 12-column classical book layout. Grid proportions reference Jan Tschichold’s canonical book page divisions, utilizing generous outer margins and balanced gutters.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout, `3rem` canvas margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Sidebar navigations (representing library stacks and classification trees) occupy 3 columns; the catalog reading area spans 9 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Stacks collapse into a drawer overlay; catalog view spans all 8 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Stacks convert into an index bottom sheet.

### Rhythmic Rules
Padding inside archival containers follows an alternating bilateral rhythm: top/bottom padding is intentionally slightly taller than side padding on card elements (`space-lg` vertical vs `space-md` horizontal), echoing historical book title and plate framing proportions.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tactile Layering Over Drop Shadows
Avoid high-blur, software-style digital shadows. Depth is achieved via **tonal materiality, fine borders, and warm micro-ambient tints**:

- **Level 0 (Desk Canvas):** `#F4EFE6` with an optional micro-grain canvas opacity layer.
- **Level 1 (Card folios, Catalog indices):** `#FCFAF6` bordered by a 1px solid `#D8CEBC` rule. Elevation is accented only by a hairline warm shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.05), 0 4px 12px rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active dossiers, Drawer trays):** `#EAE3D2` bound by a double-rule border (1px solid `#B88E3E` paired with a 2px inner inset `#FCFAF6`). Shadow: `0 6px 18px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal folios, Manuscript inspection glass):** `#FCFAF6` with an exterior border of 2px solid `#1B3B2B` and an ambient shadow: `0 16px 36px rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

### Edge Treatment & Geometry
Corners utilize minimal softness (`roundedness: 1`, where base radius is `0.25rem` / 4px). This matches the slightly softened yet crisp corners of hand-cut rag paper, leather binding boards, and archival index cards. Full circles are strictly prohibited except for circular *ex libris* wax seals and conservation stamp markers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Master Callout):** Solid British Library Green (`#1B3B2B`), text in `#FCFAF6` set in `Inter` semi-bold uppercase (`label-lg`), 1px internal border in `#B88E3E`. Hover transitions to `#132B1F` with a soft gilt border highlight.
- **Secondary (Archival Action):** Oxblood Burgundy (`#6A1A24`) fill, text `#FCFAF6`, borderless, with a pressed tactile response.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Transparent background, 1px border in `#595149`, text `#1F1A17` in `Source Serif 4`. Hover reveals a `#EAE3D2` wash.

### Cards & Folios
- Fabricated with `#FCFAF6` surface, enclosed by a 1px border `#D8CEBC`.
- Special collections feature a top border accent: a 2px horizontal stroke in `#B88E3E`.
- Top-right corner accommodates call-number plates (`Inter` 11px uppercase in a pale tinted box `#EAE3D2`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FCFAF6`, resting border 1px `#D8CEBC`.
- Placeholder text in italicized `Source Serif 4` (`#595149`).
- Focused state eliminates modern glow rings; instead, activates a sharp 1.5px border in `#1B3B2B` paired with a subtle 1px offset ring in `#B88E3E`.

### Chips & Classification Tags
- **UDC / Shelfmark Tags:** Structured rectangular chips, radius 2px, `#EAE3D2` background, text `#1F1A17` in `Inter` (`label-md`), bordered by `#D8CEBC`.
- **Preservation Status Chips (Restricted/Fragile):** Oxblood tinted background (`rgba(106, 26, 36, 0.1)`), text `#6A1A24`, surrounded by a 1px dashed `#6A1A24` border.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkboxes: 16px square, radius 2px. Unchecked: `#FCFAF6` with 1.5px `#595149` border. Checked: `#1B3B2B` with a crisp `#FCFAF6` checkmark.
- Radio buttons: Circular, unchecked bordered in `#595149`. Checked: nested brass-ring design with a solid `#1B3B2B` center dot.

### Lists & Archival Catalog Tables
- Alternating rows avoid standard gray striping; they alternate between `#F4EFE6` and `#FCFAF6`.
- Row dividers are 1px solid hairline rules in `#E2DAC9`.
- Hover triggers an illumination in pale vellum tint `#EAE3D2` with a 2px left border accent in `#B88E3E`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Ex Libris Seal Badge:** Circular seal device containing monogram icon and Latin cataloging provenance, rendered in duotone Oxblood (`#6A1A24`) with an authentic stamp-ink multiply blend.
- **Manuscript Folio Viewer:** Dual-pane parchment backdrop with a physical gutter shadow down the spine, allowing side-by-side paleographical transcription alongside high-resolution zoomable scans.
- **Call Number Spine Label:** Vertical or horizontal ticket element with heavy border rules top and bottom, set in high-contrast monospaced or dense sans-serif for quick physical shelf correlation.
